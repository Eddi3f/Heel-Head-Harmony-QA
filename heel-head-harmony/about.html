All about me

My journey into reflexology

A career change born from a passion for helping people to help themselves.

My name is Pamela and I qualified as a Level 5 reflexologist with David Wayte at Jubilee College in October 2020. I am registered with the Association of Reflexologists and fully insured with Alan Boswell.

After 28 years working in correctional services — the last 15 helping offenders with drug and alcohol problems make positive changes to their lives — I decided it was time for a change. So, in October 2019, I began training to be a reflexologist. I absolutely loved the course and the case studies. 2020 made the training particularly challenging, but made me more determined to complete it and start the next part of my journey.

I have gone on to complete further training in both finger-free reflexology with Jubilee College and, most recently, auricular reflexology with the Contemporary Reflexology College. Each day brings new learning and more opportunity to expand my knowledge.

I am passionate about helping people to help themselves, and reflexology fits well with this — your body works with me to balance and restore. I am based in Hatfield, Doncaster, and can offer treatments at my home or at Action First Physiotherapy, Snaith.
